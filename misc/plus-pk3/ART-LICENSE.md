The pictures of the Remake menu in `zzzz-plus-remake.pk3` (backgrounds, hover pictures, medals,
slides and the other textures under `textures/menu_plus/remake/`), rendered from the maintainer's
Blender scenes of the menu rooms:

- Author: OpenMOHAA Plus contributors (the maintainer).
- Licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- AI-assisted: yes. Some textures in the scenes were made with ComfyUI and FLUX.2 [klein] 9B by

  the FLUX Non-Commercial License v2.1 says Black Forest Labs claims no ownership of the Outputs;
  section 4(a) restricts commercial use of data produced by the model.
- Third-party 3D models in the scenes keep their own licences (CC BY 4.0 or CC0); they are credited
  on the chalkboard of the main room, from `remake/credits/models.json`.
- The renders are not in the repository: the maintainer builds the pak from them and attaches it to
  each release.

The fonts carry their own notices: `fonts/plus-fonts-license.txt` in `zzz-plus.pk3`, and
`fonts/pluschalk-32-OFL.txt` (Cabin Sketch, SIL Open Font License) in the Remake pak.

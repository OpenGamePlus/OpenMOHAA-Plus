# Menu art licence

The menu backgrounds of OpenMOHAA Plus, and the textures in `zzz-plus.pk3` made from them:

| File | Textures | Used by | Scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art/background.jpg` | `bg_l`, `bg_r` | main menu, server browser | operations room with a map of Europe |
| `art/background_options.jpg` | `opt_l`, `opt_r` | options | radio desk in a brick cellar |


- Author: OpenMOHAA Plus contributors (the maintainer).
- Licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- AI-generated: yes. Made with ComfyUI, workflow `OMOHPlus_Menu_Fondo_T2I.json`, model FLUX.2 [klein]
  9B (nvfp4) by Black Forest Labs, from a text prompt only (no reference image). An insignia the
  model drew in an earlier background was removed by hand. The disclosure follows section 2(e) of
  the model licence.
- Rights in the output: section 2(d) of the FLUX Non-Commercial License v2.1 says Black Forest Labs
  claims no ownership of the Outputs and that they may be used for any purpose except as the licence
  prohibits.
- Note: section 4(a) of that licence restricts commercial use of data produced by the model. If that
  ever matters, the art can be regenerated with FLUX.2 [klein] 4B (Apache 2.0).

The fonts in the same pk3 carry their own notice, `fonts/plus-fonts-license.txt`.
